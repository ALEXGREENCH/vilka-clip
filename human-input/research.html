100 demos. Human intention.

HUMAN / INPUT is a machine-made demo about the person using the machine. Its thesis is deliberately self-referential: AI is a tool. You are the reason. The soundtrack is an original, melodic four-channel chiptune with driven pulse leads, saturated triangle bass, hard drums, add9 harmony and rapid crystal arpeggios. Every sound sample is synthesized by code and stored in a real ProTracker MOD.

Research method

Snapshot: 2 October 2026. The first 100 unique productions in Pouët's demo category, sorted by popularity (order=views), four pages of 25 entries. This is one catalogue's ranking, not a universal ranking of the entire scene.

Original Pouët chart · Official API · Research JSON · ProTracker 2 clone

Metadata and production-page text were inspected for all 100 releases. All 100 full videos were not watched. “Page mentions” are words found in the page and discussion, not verified effect attribution. The work interprets scene traditions; it does not include other demos' music, footage, samples or source code.

The impossible finale

Starting at 2:18, geometry moves beyond a consistent everyday viewpoint: hypercube edges rotate through the fourth dimension; three beams occlude one another cyclically; portals repeat into their own vanishing point; a stable external camera orbits a bounded Menger sponge with recursively carved holes. The previous close-up Mandelbox was replaced after visual review. The film also adds an Amiga-style Boing Ball, fictional AI-logo parodies, and short half-beat turbo montages. These are new implementations for this film, not claims of inventing new mathematical effects.

Mathematical sources: Tom Lowe: earlier Mandelbox experiment (replaced); Seven / Fulcrum: fractal rendering background. Browser rendering uses a WebGL Menger shader when available and a software fallback; video export uses the software renderer.
